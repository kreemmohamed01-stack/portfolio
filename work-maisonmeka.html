Overview

Maison Meka sells timeless pieces chosen to bring character, beauty and elegance into a space — so the site had to feel unhurried rather than transactional. The build pairs soft neutral tones with editorial serif headings and generous white space, letting each piece hold the frame. The site is still in active development, with the storefront and collections being refined ahead of launch.

Highlights

01 Editorial Art Direction

A "curated home art" hero and warm, sunlit imagery set a gallery-like tone from the first screen.

02 Collection-Led Browsing

Pieces are grouped into collections rather than dumped into a grid, so discovery feels like walking a showroom.

03 Mobile-First Layouts

Every section was composed for the phone screen first, where most of the browsing actually happens.

04 Custom Orders Path

Alongside the shop, a dedicated route for custom orders and direct enquiries for clients who want something made for their space.
