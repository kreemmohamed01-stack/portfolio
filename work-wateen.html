Overview

Wateen Perfume needed a storefront that carries the same weight as the fragrances it sells. The site pairs bold product photography with a clean, focused layout — so every scent gets its moment before the customer moves on to checkout.

Highlights

01 Bold Product Showcase

Full-width imagery and clean product cards put every bottle front and center from the first scroll.

02 Fast, Focused Browsing

A simple, uncluttered structure keeps customers moving from discovery to checkout without friction.

03 Mobile-First Shopping

Every layout was built for the phone screen first, keeping the buying journey fast and light.

04 Connected Store Backend

Paired with a custom admin dashboard so orders, inventory, and content stay easy to manage day to day.
