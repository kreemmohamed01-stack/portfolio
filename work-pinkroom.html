Overview

The Pink Room needed a storefront that felt as considered as the pieces it sells. The site is built around full-bleed lifestyle photography, calm typography, and an unhurried scroll — so a coffee table or a vase reads like part of a home, not just a product on a shelf.

Highlights

01 Editorial Homepage

A full-screen hero and lifestyle imagery that set a refined, magazine-like tone before any product appears.

02 Curated Collections

Products are grouped into clean, browsable collections that make discovering new pieces feel effortless.

03 Mobile-First Shopping

Every layout was built for the phone screen first, keeping checkout fast and the browsing experience light.

04 Connected Store Backend

Paired with a custom admin dashboard so orders, inventory, and content stay easy to manage day to day.
