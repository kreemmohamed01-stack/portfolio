Overview

Luxe is a landing page built for a menswear brand that wanted to feel less like a store and more like a statement. The entry sequence, the pacing of the scroll, and the restrained typography are all designed to slow the visitor down and make the brand feel premium before a single product is shown.

Highlights

01 Cinematic Entry

A full-screen intro sequence that sets the tone the moment the page loads, before revealing the site.

02 Scroll-Driven Reveals

Sections fade and rise into view as the visitor scrolls, keeping the pacing deliberate and premium.

03 Minimal Art Direction

A tight black-and-white palette with a single accent, letting the photography carry the brand.

04 Responsive Lookbook

A lookbook layout that holds its composition from a large desktop screen down to a single phone column.
