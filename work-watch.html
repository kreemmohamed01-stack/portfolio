Overview

My Watch needed a site that could carry the weight of a luxury timepiece brand — precise, controlled, and confident. The layout leans on large product photography, a dark editorial palette, and quiet typography so the craftsmanship of the watch stays the focus on every screen size.

Highlights

01 Editorial Product Layout

Large-format photography treated like a print campaign, giving each piece room to breathe.

02 Collection Browsing

A clear, low-noise way to move between collections without breaking the premium feel.

03 Detail-Focused Animation

Subtle motion on hover and scroll that draws attention to the details that sell a luxury watch.

04 Responsive Dark Theme

The same dark, high-contrast mood holds up from a wide desktop display down to a phone screen.
