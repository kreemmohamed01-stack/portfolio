Overview

United Construction needed a site that carried the weight of the work it delivers — heritage restoration, GRC manufacturing and installation, and architectural development. The design leans on warm stone tones and wide project photography, with every page built to move a visitor from "who are these people" to a clear picture of what the company has actually executed on the ground.

Highlights

01 Bilingual Arabic & English

The whole site switches between Arabic and English, with the layout flipping direction so each language reads naturally rather than looking translated.

02 Project-Led Storytelling

Executed works are presented with location, project type and duration, so credibility comes from the record rather than from claims.

03 Clear Service Structure

Heritage projects, architectural development and GRC works each get their own space, making it easy for a client to find the exact service they came for.

04 Direct Contact Routes

WhatsApp and enquiry paths sit within reach on every screen, keeping the gap between interest and a conversation as short as possible.
