Overview

Antique Home deals in curated antiques and timeless pieces crafted to bring character into every space. The site is built around full-bleed lifestyle imagery and quiet, classical typography, so browsing feels closer to walking through a collection than scrolling a shop. It's still in active development, with the catalogue and checkout flow being finished ahead of launch.

Highlights

01 Cinematic Hero

A full-screen opening scene that sets the tone — "timeless pieces, endless beauty" — before a single product appears.

02 Bilingual Shopping

Arabic and English throughout, so local customers browse and check out in whichever language they think in.

03 Promotions Built In

A top-level offer bar and sale badges keep running promotions visible without cluttering the product layouts.

04 Fast Mobile Checkout

Cart, account and search sit within thumb reach, with WhatsApp support one tap away at any point in the journey.
