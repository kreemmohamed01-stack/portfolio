      Questions

      Are domain and hosting included?

      Yes — every package includes your domain and the first year of hosting. Renewal is yearly, not monthly.

      Which hosting do I need?

      Package prices include Standard hosting (1,000 EGP/year value): about 300–400 visitors a day, ~10,000 a month, with limited photos and videos. For bigger stores there’s Pro hosting (3,000 EGP/year value): up to 2,000 visitors a day, ~70,000 a month, with lots of photos and videos — it adds 2,000 EGP to any package.

      How much is the yearly renewal?

      Domain + hosting renewal is once a year: 3,000 EGP/year with Standard hosting, or 4,500 EGP/year with Pro hosting.

      What happens after launch?

      Essential and Premium include 2 weeks of free follow-up and Signature includes 1 month. After that, each edit is 500 EGP.

      Can I add more products later?

      Yes — anytime, by yourself, for free. You add products, prices and photos straight from your dashboard, with no limit and without needing me. If you’d rather I add them: 1,000 EGP per 50 on Premium, 500 EGP per 50 on Signature.

      Will I be able to manage the site myself?

      Yes — you get full control of the whole website. Your dashboard is very easy to use: add or edit products, prices, photos, categories and content yourself, whenever you want, with no coding and no extra payments. I build the dashboard around whatever you want to control. Order details go straight to your WhatsApp and email — and to your customer too.

      Which package saves me the most?

      Essential (12,000 EGP) — our most popular. You add all the products yourself from the dashboard, which is quick and very easy, so you never pay per product.

      Does the AI agent speak Arabic?

      Yes — it works in Arabic and English, reads your whole website and recommends the right products 24/7.

      I need something different.

      Choose Custom in any category. We plan it around your goals, with a timeline based on your needs.
