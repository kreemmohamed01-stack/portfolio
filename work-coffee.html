Overview

This coffee brand needed a storefront that could carry a premium, cafe-like feel online. The site pairs a dark, low-contrast palette with close-up product photography and steady motion, so the site feels as crafted as the coffee itself — whether it's opened on a laptop or a phone.

Highlights

01 Dark, Premium Art Direction

A moody dark theme with warm product photography that gives the brand an upscale, cafe-quality feel.

02 Product-First Homepage

The hero leads straight into the roasts on offer, with clear calls to action to shop each blend.

03 Fully Responsive Layout

Every section was tuned to hold its composition from a wide desktop screen down to a single phone column.

04 Fast, Focused Storefront

A lean product structure with quick navigation, built to get visitors from browsing to checkout with ease.
