Overview

Ova Perfume needed a storefront that could sell the feeling of a fragrance, not just the bottle. The site was built around large, moody visuals, a fast Easy-Order checkout flow, and a layout that keeps the shopping experience simple on mobile — where most of the brand's customers browse and buy.

Highlights

01 Easy Order Checkout

A streamlined, low-friction ordering flow built for direct WhatsApp/Easy Order sales instead of a heavy cart process.

02 Product Storytelling

Full-bleed imagery and short, confident copy that sell the mood of each fragrance before the ingredients.

03 Mobile-First Shopping

Every section was designed for the phone screen first, since that's where the vast majority of visits happen.

04 Custom Brand Visuals

Dark, editorial art direction tailored to the Ova identity — consistent across the whole shopping journey.
